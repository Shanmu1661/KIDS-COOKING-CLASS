Curriculum Overview

Where Science, Math & Nutrition Come Alive

Little Chefs Academy hosts engaging school field trips engineered collaboratively by certified science educators and professional executive chefs. Each trip bridges core curriculum concepts with tangible, edible laboratory experiments students never forget.

Our modern 4,500 sq. ft. campus accommodates cohorts from 15 up to 60 students simultaneously with rotating interactive stations. Teachers receive comprehensive pre-trip lesson prep worksheets and post-trip assessment quizzes. Chaperones and educators attend 100% free with access to our teacher work lounge.

4 Core STEM Curriculum Modules

Biology Lab
Fermentation & Yeast Ecology

Investigate microscopic Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Students test yeast gas production with balloon flasks under variable sugar and temperature controls while kneading scratch dough.

Cellular respiration & gluten elasticity
Physics Lab
Thermodynamics & Maillard Reaction

Explore conduction, convection, and electromagnetic induction heating. Observe caramelization, non-enzymatic browning, and phase transitions in baking breads.

Heat transfer & chemical change
Chemistry Lab
Emulsions, pH & Molecular Density

Create stable emulsions using natural lecithin surfactants. Test acidity and alkalinity of fresh foods using red cabbage indicator solutions and digital pH probes.

Surfactants, polar bonds & pH scale
Botany & Math
Courtyard Botany & Fraction Math

Dissect edible flower petals and herb foliage in our courtyard garden. Scale multi-yield recipes calculating proportional volume fractions and gram conversions.

Plant anatomy & applied fractions

The Field Trip Schedule

Typical 2 to 2.5-Hour Laboratory Flow

01
0:00 – 0:20: Bus Arrival, Safety & Hypothesis Briefing

Students stow backpacks, sanitize hands at sensor stations, put on lab aprons, and formulate scientific hypotheses in lab booklets.

02
0:20 – 1:00: Biological Fermentation & Dough Kneading

Active yeast respiration experiments, measuring thermal water thresholds, and kneading high-gluten bread doughs.

03
1:00 – 1:35: Courtyard Botany Tour & Herb Infusion

Harvesting rosemary, basil, and thyme in our outdoor garden; conducting sensory aroma tests and studying chlorophyll taxonomy.

04
1:35 – 2:15: Oven Bake, Maillard Evaluation & Scientific Tasting

Observing non-enzymatic browning in deck ovens, calculating volume expansion, scientific flavor tasting, and packing take-home flatbreads.

Strict School Safety & Allergen Sanctuary

Our entire academy is strictly peanut and tree-nut free. Certified in Pediatric First Aid. Cool-touch induction cooktops prevent flame or burn risks.
