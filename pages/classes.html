The Academy Promise

What's Included in Every Single Class

No hidden fees, no supply lists. We provide everything young chefs need for an inspiring, safe, and joyful culinary journey.

Zero Burn Risk

Kid-Safe Workstation

Individual cool-touch induction cooktops, child-ergonomic nylon safety knives, non-slip step stools, and sanitizing wipes. Every child works at their own dedicated counter.

100% Nut-Free

Fresh Farm Ingredients

Pre-measured organic produce, non-GMO dairy, unbleached flours, and safe allergen substitutions. Prepared fresh every morning with strict kitchen cross-contact prevention.

Complimentary embroidered apron and fabric chef toque to wear, laminated step-by-step recipe cards, and a family-sized bakery box of delicious food made in class.
