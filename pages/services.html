Peace of Mind

Safety, Sanitation & Dietary Standards

We hold the highest safety ratings in child culinary education, engineered specifically for parents' complete peace of mind.

100% Nut-Safe Sanctuary

Our entire facility is strictly tree-nut and peanut-free. All incoming wholesale flour, chocolates, and grains undergo strict supplier allergen verification.

Cut-Resistant Safety Gloves

Every student wears Level 5 cut-resistant safety gloves matched to their hand size alongside ergonomic, rounded-tip safety blades.

Cool-Touch Induction Cooktops

Zero open gas flames. Our commercial magnetic induction burners heat cookware directly while the surrounding surface remains cool to the touch.

Color-Coded Dietary Stations

Color-coded prep boards and dedicated utensils protect students with gluten, dairy, or egg sensitivities from cross-contact.

ServSafe Certified Chefs

All instructors maintain active ServSafe Manager credentials, background checks, and pediatric CPR/First-Aid certifications.

Medical-Grade UV Sterilization

All workstations, cutting mats, and chef implements pass through commercial high-temp wash cycles and UV sterilization chambers before every class.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

Everything you need to know about enrollment, dietary safety, and kitchen logistics.

We are a designated nut-free facility. We strictly forbid any tree nuts, peanuts, peanut oils, or ingredients packaged in facilities that process nuts. Every vendor invoice and raw ingredient shipment is vetted by our Culinary Safety Director before entering the kitchen.

Single drop-ins are standalone weekend classes where kids master a single fun recipe (e.g. handmade pasta or macarons). Full 8-week semesters follow a progressive pedagogical curriculum where students build cumulative skills (knife safety, heat regulation, mother sauces, culinary math) and graduate with a personalized chef apron and certificate.

For our Tiny Tasters (ages 3–5), one parent or guardian participates side-by-side with the child at a shared station. For all older programs (Little Chefs, Junior Gourmet, Teen Masterclass, and Summer Camps), classes are drop-off! Parents can relax in our viewing lounge with free WiFi and organic coffee, or return at pick-up time.

We understand childhood illnesses and family calendar conflicts happen! Drop-in classes can be rescheduled up to 48 hours in advance through the Parent Portal for 100% account credit. For 8-week semester students, we offer up to 2 flexible makeup sessions per semester during designated weekend makeup windows.

Weekend birthday party slots are in high demand and typically book 4 to 8 weeks in advance. We recommend contacting us as early as possible to secure your preferred Saturday or Sunday morning/afternoon slot. A 50% deposit secures the date.
