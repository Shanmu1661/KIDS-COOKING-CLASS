Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your child’s first culinary session.

Every student is provided with an ergonomic child-safe nylon or safety-shield blade designed to easily cut vegetables without piercing skin. All cooking surfaces utilize induction heat technology that remains cool to the touch, eliminating open flame risks.

Our entire facility is strictly tree-nut and peanut-free. When you register your child in our Parent Portal, you can enter specific allergy alerts (dairy, gluten, egg, etc.). Our chefs prepare dedicated color-coded workstations and allergen-free ingredient substitutes accordingly.

For our Tiny Tasters (Ages 3-5), parent participation is welcomed and encouraged! For older age groups (6-17), parents are welcome to relax in our viewing lounge equipped with comfortable seating, complimentary organic coffee, high-speed WiFi, and large viewing windows overlooking the kitchen.

We understand family schedules change! Single weekend classes can be rescheduled up to 48 hours in advance directly from your Parent Dashboard with full credit refunded to your account. Summer camps can be cancelled up to 14 days before camp start for a 100% refund.
