Daily Routine

A Typical Day at Culinary Camp

Balanced between high-energy kitchen creation, outdoor garden breaks, and tasting celebrations.

9:00 AM – Arrival & Morning Chef Briefing

Campers don their personalized embroidered aprons, review the day's recipe cards, and explore food science chemistry concepts of the day.

9:30 AM – Mise en Place & Knife Skill Stations

Hands-on preparation of all ingredients using ergonomic child-safe cutting tools. Measuring dry and wet ingredients accurately.

11:00 AM – Cooking & Sautéing Lab

Working at their cool-touch induction burners, students execute the primary hot recipe (e.g. handmade pasta sauce, stir-fries, or artisan bread baking).

12:30 PM – Community Chef Family Lunch

Students gather at our family banquet table to enjoy the meal they prepared together. Discussion on texture, spice adjustments, and culinary vocabulary.

1:30 PM – Herb Garden Exploration & Dessert Creation

Afternoon baking session (cupcakes, tarts, cookies) with take-home bakery packaging so parents can taste the results at home!

3:00 PM – Daily Badge Ceremony & Parent Pick-up

Each camper is awarded their daily culinary badge and a bakery box filled with their afternoon creations!
