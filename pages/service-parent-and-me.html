Workshop Overview

Unplug, Bond & Create Delicious Memories Together

Step side-by-side up to a shared spacious culinary island. Cook authentic handmade cuisine together with zero prep, zero stress, and zero dishes to clean!

Modern family life can be hectic, but cooking together slows down time. In our Parent & Me workshops, you and your child operate as a collaborative kitchen duo. Guided by our gentle master chefs, you'll discover the art of handmade gnocchi, Bavarian soft pretzels, delicate dim sum dumplings, and artisan French pastries.

Any loving adult is warmly welcome—moms, dads, grandparents, aunts, uncles, or mentors! Best of all, our team preps all ingredients in advance and cleans every bowl and pan, so your entire morning or afternoon is devoted to laughter, learning, and dining together.
