Celebration Overview

The Ultimate Hands-On Culinary Birthday Celebration

Give your child a one-of-a-kind birthday party where young guests cook, laugh, taste, and celebrate together—while parents relax in our viewing lounge with zero dishes to wash!

Our birthday party package grants your group exclusive buyout access to our modern culinary studio. Led by two high-energy chef entertainers, children don personalized paper chef hats, stretch yeasted pizza dough into creative shapes, ladle scratch herb marinara sauce, and pipe vibrant cupcakes with artisanal frosting bags.

While the children cook, adult guests are treated to our glass-enclosed coffee lounge with complimentary organic espresso, loose-leaf teas, and chilled sparkling water. Every child leaves with a full belly, party favor pastry box, and joyful memories!

The Party Flow

How the 2-Hour Birthday Celebration Unfolds

01
0:00 – 0:15: Red Carpet Arrival & Chef Hat Decorating

Party guests arrive, wash hands at our sensor sanitizing stations, write names on paper chef hats, and take group red-carpet photos.

02
0:15 – 0:55: Pizza Dough Stretching & Cooktop Sauces

Flour flies! Kids roll yeasted dough into rounds, ladle scratch simmered marinara sauce, layer organic mozzarella, and bake in pizza ovens.

03
0:55 – 1:25: Cupcake Decorating Bakery Studio

Learning professional piping bag grips, swirling buttercream stars and rosettes, and customizing with natural sprinkles.

04
1:25 – 2:00: Hot Pizza Feast, Birthday Singing & Favors

Dining on hot fresh pizzas, singing Happy Birthday around the candle display, packing bakery take-home boxes, and celebratory send-off.
