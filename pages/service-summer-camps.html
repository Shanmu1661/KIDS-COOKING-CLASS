Hands-On Summer Kitchen Camp
Camp Overview

Unforgettable 5-Day Kitchen Adventures

Campers cook complete hot 2-course lunches every single day, explore edible food science, and host a magnificent Friday graduation banquet for their families!

Our summer camp program runs Monday through Friday in convenient full-day (9am–3pm) and morning half-day (9am–12pm) formats. Every week features a unique, deeply engaging theme—from Hand-Rolled Pasta & French Boulangerie to Global Street Food Journeys and Junior Iron Chef Mystery Basket challenges.

Children are grouped by age tier (Ages 6–9, 10–12, and 13–16) with dedicated chef educators maintaining a safe 1:5 mentor ratio. Every camper leaves with newly forged culinary independence, lifelong food enthusiasm, and an embroidered keepsake chef apron.

4 Immersive Weekly Camp Themes

Theme 1
Global Street Food Fiesta

Journey around the culinary world: authentic Mexican street tacos & churros, hand-pleated Japanese dumplings, Greek souvlaki with tzatziki, and Indian samosas.

Cultural gastronomy & spices
Theme 2
Junior Iron Chef & Pastry Wars

Exciting culinary challenges, mystery basket recipe invention, piping French macarons, blindfold aroma recognition games, and artistic plating battles.

Creative problem solving & baking
Theme 3
STEM Food Lab & Molecular Chemistry

Investigate sourdough wild yeast fermentation, calcium-alginate fruit boba spherification, emulsification science, and the physics of candy glass.

Edible chemistry & science experiments
Theme 4
Farm-to-Table & Herb Courtyard

Daily harvesting from our sunny outdoor courtyard raised beds, plant anatomy, scratch hand-cranked pasta, herb focaccias, and farmer's market galettes.

Botany, ecology & fresh harvesting

Camp Daily Rhythm

A Day in the Life of a Little Chef Camper (9 AM – 3 PM)

01
9:00 AM – Morning Kitchen Huddle & Pastry Bakery Lab

Hand hygiene, donning uniforms, reviewing the day's culinary roadmap, and baking breakfast scones, brioche buns, or fruit galettes.

02
10:45 AM – Courtyard Botany Harvest & Ingredient Prep

Snip fresh herbs in the courtyard, practice safe knife cuts (julienne, dice, mince), and prep fresh produce for savory lunch.

03
11:30 AM – Savory Induction Cooking & Communal Feast

Chefs activate cooktops to simmer, sautÃ©, and sear their hot 2-course meal, followed by dining together at our family harvest table.

04
1:30 PM – Mystery Basket Challenge, Science Lab & Pack Up

Edible food science experiments, plating battles, recording reflections in their recipe binders, and packing afternoon baked goods.

100% Nut-Safe & Allergy-Guaranteed Camp

Our entire facility is tree-nut and peanut-free. All culinary staff are certified in Pediatric First Aid and Epipen administration. Customized allergy substitutes provided seamlessly.
